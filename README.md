# What is a website:


My website is about me, and it is more or less about how I wish I could be a cloud, just flying over the world peacefully. The website is about that—it is simple and easy to use; you just press the buttons. It has three tabs, and they are about different things, talking about clouds. But at the same time that it talks about me, it also shares facts, curiosities, and information about clouds.

Just one detail: the text might look like AI or feel a bit strange because I used Google Translate, so some words and phrases might look a bit off



# What information can be brought:


it brings things about clouds—their information and their life cycle. The website is interesting because of that; it talks about their importance, why they are useful, and it shows people cool facts about them. It doesn’t have too much stuff, but it is worth seeing.
